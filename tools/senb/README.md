# SENB sentence builder

Serve `index.html`, `manifest.webmanifest`, `sw.js`, `assets/`, `images/`, and `sounds/` together under the existing SENB URL, over HTTPS. No runtime build, CDN JavaScript, framework, or backend is required. The optional Google font falls back to the system font when unavailable.

## Files

- `index.html`: Arabic RTL markup and toolbar/dialog structure.
- `assets/styles.css`: card styling, responsive toolbar, safe areas, scroll containers, and touch targets.
- `assets/utilities.css`: committed, compiled Tailwind utility CSS. Browsers do not compile styles at runtime.
- `assets/data.js`: vocabulary, conjugations, advanced categories, image-folder map, and all 960 advanced sentence presets. Presets are constructed once at startup.
- `assets/grammar.js`: context-dependent titles, gender agreement, slot colors, and image/audio path resolution.
- `assets/app.js`: session state, rendering, pickers, keyboard support, viewport sizing, and shared audio playback.
- `assets/activities-model.js`: random assessment plans, game choices, and isolated grammar lookup for activity sentences.
- `assets/activities.js`: home activity setup, repetition playback, comprehension/expressive scoring, and pointer/touch game controls.
- `assets/activities.css`: responsive home menu, activity cards, stars, assessment choices, and game layout.
- `assets/image-fallback.svg`: local placeholder for unavailable images.

The rows of `ADV_IMAGE_FOLDER_MAP` in `data.js` correspond to sentence groups 2–6; each array lists the image folders for subgroups 1–8. Audio retains the requested sentence group/subgroup path. Do not infer the audio path from the image folder.

Slot selection, preset slot assignments, advanced title overrides, noun gender lookup, verb subject lookup, pronoun modifiers, and all conjugation data were preserved during extraction. Image thumbnails load on demand; startup does not download the entire image/audio library. The PWA caches the app interface and saves media as it is used. App installation is available from the home-screen header icon.

Verbs scan earlier slots in the same row for the nearest valid personal pronoun, subject, object, or place. Gaps, adjectives, colors, and other modifiers are skipped. A noun’s explicit `genderCode` takes priority over its gender; the preset verb’s `conjugation` is used when no earlier context is available. Dynamic labels, audio paths, and both future clips share this lookup. Explicit preset title overrides stay intact.

Future verb slot audio is composed as two consecutive present-folder clips: `15.m4a` (want), then the selected verb's `1.m4a`–`100.m4a`, using the same subject code. The slot's future-tense label and advanced whole-sentence recordings remain unchanged. A new tap cancels the rest of an interrupted two-clip phrase.

## Activities

The home menu opens the original sentence board, repetition tasks, assessments, or the missing-card game. Activities read the original presets; they do not replace the manual board's slot assignments or its grammar settings.

- Repetition: select 2–6 words and a subgroup. Each repetition plays the advanced recording followed by all its contextual slot recordings. Repeat the complete sequence 1–5 times; navigate manually or use automatic advancement. One of 24 stars lights after each completed sentence. Pause, a card tap, or navigation cancels pending playback and advancement.
- Comprehension: choose sentence length, one subgroup or all subgroups, and advanced images or word-card rows. Each selected subgroup contributes three distinct random targets from its 24 sentences, with four visually distinct alternatives per trial. Choices have no sentence titles or word circles. The first answer is recorded, then the test advances. Replay is available before answering; the result shows the percentage of correct answers for the selected scope: three trials for one subgroup, or 24 for all eight.
- Expressive: choose sentence length, one subgroup or all subgroups, and advanced images or word-card rows. Each selected subgroup contributes the chosen number of distinct random targets (three by default). Toggle one circle per spoken word, then use the next arrow. The result shows the percentage of marked words out of all possible words across the selected trials.
- Game: random sentences from the chosen word group without repeats until the 192-sentence bag is exhausted. One mapped slot is missing, with the correct card and two alternatives from its vocabulary category. Drag a card into the missing slot, or tap a card and then tap the slot. Images/cards play their recordings; the hint plays the missing word with the sentence's tense/pronoun context.

Repetition uses 1–5 count buttons above icon-only playback/navigation controls. Repetition cards keep their 16:9 shape. Active missing-card games and expressive trials size images and controls to the available viewport without scrolling, including a two-column layout on short landscape screens. Game setup supports the entire sentence-length group or multiple subgroups; each repeated game bag keeps that selection. The homepage footer contains Ahmad Ghanem's professional/contact information.

Assessment screens keep their existing percentage/MLU summaries and PDF export. Completed assessments also save dated evidence in the active child’s local profile, available through the period report icon. Starting a new activity resets its live results while retaining saved history.

Some legacy presets use alternate category spellings. Activities resolve these on temporary copies, preserving the stored data and the workplace's grammar context. Twelve existing slot references have no supplied vocabulary mapping: `UNKNOWN` entries in `2/7/20`, `3/2/16`, `4/2/5`, `4/2/19`, `6/8/11`; object `162` in `4/6/15`, `4/6/16`, `5/6/15`, `5/6/16`, `6/6/15`, `6/6/16`; and verb `0` in `6/7/22`. They display the local placeholder and use Arabic speech synthesis when available. The game chooses its missing slot from mapped cards. No replacement image is guessed for these unresolved entries.

## Development

From this directory, install development dependencies with `npm install` only when rebuilding CSS or running browser tests. If HTML or JavaScript utility classes change, run `npm run build:css` and commit `assets/utilities.css` with the source changes. Existing deployments use the committed CSS directly. After any app, CSS, image, or sound change, run `npm run build:pwa` and deploy the regenerated `assets/offline-assets.json` and `assets/pwa-release.js` with the changed files. Run the PWA build after the CSS build. The build hashes file contents and generates the media revision catalog and shell integrity checks; it does not modify media or vocabulary.

`node tests/offline-download.cjs` independently verifies six-file concurrency, pause/resume, saved-file reuse, validation, and audio ranges.

`npm test` runs data/grammar regression, verb-context checks, audio lifecycle checks, activity planning checks, and offline storage/catalog checks. The SHA-256 values in `tests/baseline.json` were captured from the complete app immediately before this refactor; they cover vocabulary, all 960 advanced image/audio/title/slot records, and 12,732 grammar/audio/color outputs across both rows. The original grammar digest is retained as `grammar`; `grammarAfterSubjectScan` records the intentional backward subject lookup correction on 2026-10-09. That correction changed 330 verb audio paths in the baseline, preserving all displayed preset titles, tenses, verb IDs, non-verb outputs, and data/image mappings. The regression test normalizes only the reviewed WebP/M4A extension changes and the physical prepositions-folder capitalization before comparing the original advanced/grammar digests. It still compares vocabulary data directly, including the original image-folder map. Update baselines only after an intentional, reviewed content or grammar change.

For browser checks, run `npx playwright install chromium webkit`, then `npm run test:browser`. On macOS an existing Chrome installation is used automatically. `SENB_CHROME` can select another Chrome executable; `SENB_SKIP_WEBKIT=1` runs only Chrome. `SENB_PLAYWRIGHT` can point to a separately installed Playwright package. Tests use a temporary local HTTP server and leave screenshots in `/private/tmp` on macOS. Adjust the screenshot path for another operating system if needed.

Browser coverage includes 320×568, 390×844, 844×390, 768×1024, 1024×768, 820×1180, and 1366×768 in Chromium and WebKit, plus viewport rotation while preserving slot assignments. It checks startup requests, toolbar bounds/touch targets, grammar filters, covers, clearing, advanced mappings, real M4A decoding after a user tap, and sentence playback order. WebKit desktop emulation does not replace a final check on physical iOS/iPadOS devices.

Activity browser coverage runs complete comprehension and expressive sessions for one subgroup and all subgroups on phone and tablet sizes in both engines, including subgroup selection/reset, percentage denominators, first-answer scoring, three-trial subgroup boundaries, circle toggling, advanced-image and slot-only presentations, repetition audio order and repeat count, progress stars, automatic play/pause, game hints, wrong/correct touch placement, captured pointer dragging, and navigation cancellation. The activity layouts are also checked in landscape and desktop sizes.

## Installation and saved media

Open SENB over HTTPS and press the app-install icon in the home-screen header. On Android/desktop, it opens the native install prompt when available. Otherwise it shows browser installation instructions. On iPhone/iPad, use Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen → Add. The icon is disabled when the app is already installed.

Images and sounds cache automatically when used. The download icon in the header opens a full-library download panel with file/byte progress and pause/resume. It saves six files per batch, retries transient failures up to twice, times out stalled requests, and skips already verified revisions when resumed or reopened. Shell installation also uses batches of six and remains atomic. Startup caches the interface, manifest, media revision catalog, and installation icons; it does not fetch thousands of media files. After the panel confirms completion, every catalog image and M4A recording can be used offline while browser storage remains available. Keep the app open during download; if a phone suspends it, reopen and resume. Browser storage can be cleared or evicted; persistence is requested where supported. Unmapped legacy words still rely on the existing placeholder/device speech fallback. Media responses are verified against file size and SHA-256 before caching, and cached audio supports byte ranges for Safari/iPad playback.

While online, SENB checks for updates when opened or returned to the foreground. An update icon appears in the home-screen header when a complete release is ready. Choosing it reloads into that release. A waiting update leaves the working release in place; a failed installation keeps the previous offline app. Updates reuse unchanged cached media and remove obsolete revisions. Cache names and service-worker scope are restricted to SENB, so other apps on the same website keep their own workers/data.

The existing missing recordings and unresolved preset slots listed above remain missing. Arabic speech synthesis fallback depends on voices available on the device. Completed assessment evidence is saved locally for the active child; it is not synchronized to a server.

## PWA files and hosting

- `manifest.webmanifest`, `assets/icons/`: Arabic app identity, home-screen launch, and installation icons derived from the existing logo.
- `assets/pwa.js`, `assets/pwa.css`: header installation icon, instructions/native prompt, and update control.
- `assets/offline-store.js`: shared media verification, revision keys, six-file download queue, cache inspection, and audio range responses.
- `sw.js`: scoped shell/media serving, complete-release activation, and safe cache cleanup.
- `scripts/build-pwa.cjs`: repeatable build of `assets/offline-assets.json` and `assets/pwa-release.js`. Commit both generated files.

Use HTTPS in production; localhost works for development. Opening `index.html` directly with `file://` does not enable the PWA features. Serve `.webp` as `image/webp`, `.m4a` as `audio/mp4`, `.webmanifest` as `application/manifest+json`, and `.js` as JavaScript. Avoid rewriting missing files to an HTML success page. Configure `sw.js`, `assets/pwa-release.js`, `index.html`, and the manifest to revalidate (`Cache-Control: no-cache`). Deploy a complete generated release together and invalidate stale CDN copies of changed app files. No server-side code or runtime npm installation is required. [Installation requirements](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/Progressive_web_apps/Guides/Making_PWAs_installable)

The PWA browser suite downloads the entire catalog through the header button under a nested GitHub Pages path, reopens with the origin stopped, decodes a real cached recording, checks audio ranges, and simulates successful/failed updates with a one-file media change. It also verifies that other website apps/caches remain outside SENB's scope. Chromium additionally uses browser offline emulation. WebKit uses a stopped origin because Playwright's offline flag rejects service-worker responses in the pinned browser; see [upstream issue](https://github.com/microsoft/playwright/issues/42775). Physical iPhone/iPad installation still needs a device check after deployment.

## Child sessions and home practice

SENB shares the saved child identity with VOC through the same browser storage. The VOC home header links to SENB, and SENB’s header links back to VOC; opening either keeps the active child. After the first child, a fresh simple addition/subtraction calculation protects account creation and switching in both apps. The colorful keypad supports tapping, keyboard input and Arabic digits; cancellation or a wrong answer leaves the current child unchanged. Each app stores its own workspace, evidence and activities without replacing the other app's data. Profiles and reports remain local to this browser/device.

The workplace defaults to the first sentence in the first two-word subgroup. Pressing an advanced group expands all its subgroup choices. Selecting a subgroup collapses the list and retains the chosen subgroup as a green card beneath the group; arrows browse the subgroup without requiring playback. Two learned icons in each picker show copies of cards/sentences that achieved 80% in comprehension or expression. Every item stays available in its ordinary category.

The workplace test icon introduces every visible sentence/word card with its sound, a glowing cue, an animated white hand and a green tick. A colorful 1–2–3 countdown precedes the assessment. Test prompts use a random order; errors replay the same prompt, and corrections retain the first-answer error. After a sentence, icon buttons choose another sentence or continue to automatic practice.

After each dynamic test, the green check starts the automatic plan, the repeat icon returns to the editable workplace to choose another sentence while retaining completed sentences, and the cross cancels the plan without changing the selected cards. Guided selections have a one-second gap. Missing-word trials first play the target word and demonstrate dragging the correct card into the gap; comprehension trials play the sentence and demonstrate selecting its matching choice. Each model is followed by a 1–2–3 countdown and a separate child turn. Missing-word tests accept pointer dragging or tapping. Expression models play the whole illustrated sentence, or the word cards from right to left, followed by one Taffaha-style metronome circle/click per word. The circles sit below the picture/cards and become word-response markers after the countdown. Tapping the sentence frame replays the words followed by the beats; tapping an individual card plays only that word. Demonstrations never score a response. Pausing a demonstration cancels its sound, hand, and beat highlights; resuming repeats that model before the child turn.

The automatic plan uses exactly the sentences worked on: ten complete repetitions of each, one missing-word task each, then one trial per sentence for comprehension with whole images, comprehension with word-card rows, expression with whole images, and expression with word-card rows. Expression is scored by the adult marking each word said. A genuinely custom card row has no corresponding whole-scene image, so it uses the word-card assessments; no unrelated image is assigned. Pausing, changing profiles, switching apps, or reloading interrupts audio and retains the current session for the home resume icon.

Completed assessments save dated sentence/word evidence. Practice and incomplete stages do not earn mastery. The report icon offers all-time, six-month, three-month, thirty-day, seven-day and today filters, comprehension/expression details for each image format, and an offline Arabic PDF. The family icon opens the automatically filled, editable home-play lesson with copy/share controls; see [HOME-LESSON.md](HOME-LESSON.md).

New behavior lives in `child-progress.js`, `child-ui.js`, `session-model.js`, `session.js` and `session-reports.js`, with local styles. Run `npm run test:sessions` for profile, planning and report checks, and `npm run test:sessions:browser` for picker, cross-app profiles, complete sessions and reports. Browser checks can use `SENB_PLAYWRIGHT` to locate Playwright. `tests/session-edge-browser.cjs` covers custom/duplicate cards and accepts `SENB_ENGINE=webkit`.

The older `npm test` currently encounters pre-existing baseline drift: the checked-in data/advanced/grammar hashes already disagree with `tests/baseline.json`. Two existing `.webp` files (`images/02 verbs/15.webp` and `images/logo.webp`) also contain PNG data, which the older media-format assertion rejects. These source data/media files and baselines were retained during this session-flow change.

`tests/dynamic-browser.cjs` covers the modeled activity flows, blocked-audio recovery, wrong/correct drag placement, scoring, metronome order/count, responsive layouts, and pause/reload cancellation in Chromium and WebKit. Parent account checks in both apps use three unique answer buttons for each fresh equation.

In expression card mode, pressing the frame animates and highlights each spoken card in right-to-left order; pressing one card animates only that word. This feedback is shared by the standalone expression test and the guided session. Stopping, changing trials, or leaving clears the animation. Reduced-motion settings keep the border highlight without card movement. Run `npm run test:expression:browser` (or with `SENB_ENGINE=webkit`) for synchronized speech, individual taps, interruption, keyboard access, errors, and unchanged scoring.

The workplace rhythm control replaces the add-line (+) control. It toggles a white frame with one colored metronome circle per filled first-row word, excluding empty slots. Press the frame (or Enter/Space while focused) to play its locally generated clicks from right to left, or press one circle for a single beat. Adding/removing cards and choosing another sentence update the count; hiding the row, editing, speech playback, backgrounding, and navigation cancel pending beats. The toggle is saved per child's workspace. Older saved add-line preferences enable this rhythm row; saved second-row data is retained but is no longer displayed or included in new sentence snapshots. `npm run test:workplace:browser` checks counting, rhythm order, edits, persistence and layout in Chromium; `SENB_ENGINE=webkit` runs WebKit.

Sentence progress reports show only sentence-type and subgroup details; individual sentence titles and word diagnostics are omitted from the report and PDF. Standalone test reports also omit word-type diagnostics. Family practice notes retain their separate personalized content. Acquisition charts count unique catalog sentences meeting the existing 80% threshold in the latest complete assessment per skill within the selected period, across both image presentations. Repeating a sentence does not increase acquisition; later tests can lower it. Each subgroup has 24 sentences; each type (2–6 words) has eight subgroups, totaling 192 sentences: 1/24 = 4.17% subgroup progress and 24/192 = 12.5% type progress. Only worked types/subgroups appear. Custom sentences are listed together without being assigned to catalog subgroups. Word-only responses do not enter sentence accuracy. The identical local `assets/acquisition-charts.js`/`.css` copies in VOC and SENB render accessible charts/tables and PDF charts offline.
