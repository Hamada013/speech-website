Information for Parents

Welcome! This tool is designed to help with building sentences in a fun and interactive way.

How to use:

  1. Click "Let's Start" to choose a sentence template (e.g., 1 main card + 3 word cards).
  2. Click the "+" on the big card to add a scene or main idea.
  3. Click the "+" on the small cards to add words (verbs, subjects, etc.) from specific categories.
  4. Once cards are added, you can click the "dots" icon on any card to edit its title or record your own voice.
  5. Use the "Play All" button to hear your sentence spoken out loud.
