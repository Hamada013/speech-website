Parents Guide

Goal

Drag and drop the cards to arrange the story in the correct order. This helps develop logical thinking and sequencing skills.

Tools

  • Check: Verifies if the order is correct.
  • Solve: Automatically arranges cards in the right order.
  • Play: Reads the story aloud card by card.
  • Sound: Activates prerecorded files.
  • Cover: Hides images to test memory.
  • Reset: Shuffles cards to start over.
