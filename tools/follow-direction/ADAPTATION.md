# Follow Direction / اتبع التعليمات

An Arabic-first, clinician-led PWA for play-based following-directions activities for children under six. The activity bank is original Arabic material inspired by the **task structures**, not a translation or reproduction, of Kathryn J. Tomlin, *Just for Adults: Following Directions*, LinguiSystems, 2007 (user-supplied PDF, 41 pages).

The source targets adults. This app is not a standardized preschool assessment, does not use the book's adult scores, and makes no diagnostic, developmental-age or discharge claim. The source itself describes screening as observation rather than a test (printed pp. 4–6). Select or skip tasks according to the child's comprehension, language variety, hearing, vision, attention and motor access. Three-step and conditional tasks are optional challenges, not expectations for every preschool child.

| Source, printed pages (PDF page = printed page + 1) | Preschool adaptation |
| --- | --- |
| 7–11, one/two/three-step body movements | Familiar accessible actions; observe order. No hopping, balance, finger snapping, induced coughing or fine-motor demands. |
| 12–17, concrete/abstract and multi-component directions | Familiar pictured objects, colors, shapes and size. Exclude adult vocabulary, superlatives and abstract concepts. |
| 18–20, varied instructions/numbers | Oral directions, visually countable sets of 1–3. No reading, written numbers, calculations or clock/calendar knowledge. |
| 21–22, conditional directions | Visible real-toy conditions; begin with a true condition. Two alternatives appear in practice only. |
| 23–29, pictures and multi-step instructions | Pointing and toy placement instead of writing, drawing, letters or complex markings. |
| 30–38, words, written directions, lists and maps | Omitted literacy and street/compass work. Replaced with two concrete toy actions and in/on/under/beside relationships. |

## Use and recording

- 11 skills, each with 3 screening items and 20 practice items: 33 screening tasks and 220 practice tasks (253 total). All labels/prompts are Arabic; English product name is Follow Direction.
- An adult reads the prompt. Optional numbered recordings may be added later. With recordings absent, the displayed prompt remains fully usable. Built-in SVG pictures require no external assets; real-object tasks have a book icon showing materials.
- Read once for independent screening. Any repeat, demonstration, cue, adapted motor response or retry must be recorded with the repeat/help control or the assisted score. Screen options are not self-narrating, so a child cannot obtain the answer by hearing each option.
- Choice tasks compare the first selection; ordered tasks require the exact sequence. A retry permanently marks that item as assisted even if subsequently correct. A wrong response can be recorded as incorrect without requiring correction.
- Clinicians score real-world actions as independent, assisted, incorrect or not assessed. For movement limitations, use an accessible response and record assistance or skip; do not interpret motor difficulty as a comprehension diagnosis.
- Only the latest **completed** screening controls the plan. Three independent responses out of three in a skill mark it checked/faded. Any help, error or skipped item leaves it available. A partial new screen leaves the previous completed plan intact. Checked cards can be deliberately opened for practice after confirmation. Practice never changes the screening plan.
- Existing task IDs and audio numbers 001–066 are retained; new recordings use 067–253. Saved sessions retain their original item lists; newly started practice sessions have 20 tasks per skill.
- Save after each action, cue, answer and score. Closing/reloading preserves the current child's unfinished session. Home > Follow/متابعة resumes it. Only one unfinished session per child; ending early saves a partial report. Practice and screening are separate dated sessions, never combined into a mastery score.
- Reports show counts with explicit assessed and unassessed denominators, grouped horizontal bars and the practice plan. Browser printing offers PDF. The Accounts spreadsheet icon exports all saved child cases to a local research .xlsx workbook with stable numeric case IDs, six sheets and three native charts. Screening and practice remain separate; skipped and pending items are not incorrect responses.

## Accounts and privacy

VOC-style local accounts: stable outlined fruit/animal avatars, Arabic search and sorting, protected hidden Hamada account, parent calculation gate, admin-only deletion and confirmation. Type `Hamada` to sign in to the admin account. This app has independent stores, `follow-direction-children-v1` and `follow-direction-account-avatars-v1`; it reads no VOC progress and uses no Google, Drive, Sheets, Firebase or analytics service.

These are device/browser profiles, not authenticated online accounts. They do not synchronize between devices. From the profile dialog, download a JSON backup, transfer it yourself, then restore on the second device. Restore is an explicit whole-app replacement with confirmation. Browser data clearing removes local progress; keep backups. A stale tab cannot overwrite another tab's saved changes. Corrupt/unavailable storage is reported without resetting it.
