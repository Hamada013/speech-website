Information for Parents

Welcome! This tool is designed to help with speech and language practice in a fun and interactive way.

How to use:

  1. Click "Let's Start" to choose a template (how many cards you want).
  2. Click the "+" on an empty card to add an image.
  3. You can choose from categories, search, or upload your own picture.
  4. Once cards are added, you can click the "dots" icon on a card to edit its title or record your own voice for it.
  5. Click any card to see it larger and hear the sound.
  6. Use the "Auto" button to start a "Test" or "Practice" game.
