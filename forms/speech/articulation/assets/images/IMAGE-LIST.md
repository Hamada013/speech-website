## Notes

- `47.png` represents luck (حظ). This is abstract and may not elicit a reliable picture label. Use a suitable alternative or a contextual prompt and record the method.
- The child-only picture view hides the word and scoring controls.
- A missing image has a readable placeholder. If the clinician changes to a different word, the old picture is hidden.
- See [LEVANTINE-REFERENCE.md](../LEVANTINE-REFERENCE.md) for target-by-target reference sounds and positions.
- Error scoring is disabled when the selected reference has no corresponding target sound. Choosing a local form with a different consonant requires scoring against that actual consonant.

- `3.png` now represents water (ماء), replacing the previous sky picture. Use a glass or bottle of clear water.
