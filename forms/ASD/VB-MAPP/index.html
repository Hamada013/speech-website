VB-MAPP Assessment

VB-MAPP Level 1 Guide

The VB-MAPP (Verbal Behavior Milestones Assessment and Placement Program) is designed to assess language, learning, and social skills of children with autism or other developmental disabilities. Level 1 evaluates foundational communication and social behaviors typically observed in children up to 18 months of age.

Core Domains in Level 1

  • Mand: Requesting desired items or actions.
  • Tact: Labeling or naming objects, actions, or events.
  • Listener: Responding to spoken language (receptive skills).
  • VP/MTS: Visual perceptual and matching-to-sample tasks.
  • Play: Independent and interactive play skills.
  • Social: Social engagement and interaction skills.
  • Imitation: Motor and verbal imitation skills.
  • Echoic: Repeating sounds or words.
  • Vocal: Vocal output and early speech sounds.

Identifying Information

VB-MAPP Level 1 Assessment

Mand

1. Requests preferred item spontaneously
Instructions: Observe if the child mands without echoic prompts.
2. Requests missing item when needed
Instructions: Example — puzzle missing a piece, child should request it.
3. Uses appropriate mand for attention
Instructions: Example — says "come here" or signs for attention.
4. Requests help appropriately
Instructions: When struggling with a task, child asks for help instead of whining.
5. Mands with new vocabulary items
Instructions: Observe if the child uses a new word or sign to mand for a novel item.
