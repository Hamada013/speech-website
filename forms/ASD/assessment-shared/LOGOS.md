# App logo assets

Generated with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-11-02. These are independent app identities, not official instrument logos or endorsement marks.

Final project assets:

- `../BALF/assets/icons/logo.webp` — communication book and growing speech bubbles.
- `../M-CHAT/assets/icons/logo.webp` — adult and toddler speech bubbles.

Each folder also retains `logo-source.png` and contains 16/32px favicons, a 180px Apple touch icon, 192/512px standard icons, and a padded 512px maskable icon. No existing logo was overwritten.

## Final prompt set

BLAF: “Create a polished friendly app logo for a child communication observation app, no text or letters. A small open picture book whose two pages form two softly rounded speech bubbles, with three gentle rising leaf shapes suggesting learning. Simple memorable flat illustration with slightly hand-drawn warmth, dark blue outlines, sage green and muted turquoise, ivory background. Centered bold silhouette readable as a tiny favicon. Square 1024x1024, generous 20 percent safe margin, opaque full background, no gradients outside the central motif, no puzzle pieces, no medical cross. This is the independent BLAF observation app branding, not an official instrument endorsement.”

M-CHAT: “Create a friendly independent app logo for a toddler developmental screening record app, no text, no letters. Two warm rounded speech bubbles meeting gently, one larger sage green and one smaller pale blue; inside the larger bubble a simple caring adult face and inside the smaller a happy toddler face looking toward the adult, very minimal flat illustration with dark blue outlines, cream background. A small warm gold star between the bubbles suggests attention and connection. Clear iconic silhouette, gentle pediatric feel, not an official M-CHAT instrument logo and no diagnostic claims. Centered square 1024x1024, all meaningful artwork within central60percent safe zone for maskable PWA icon. No puzzle pieces, no red cross, no gradients, no tiny details.”

Icon sizes were derived locally with Sharp; the maskable version adds padding around the source artwork. Preserve source files when regenerating sizes.
